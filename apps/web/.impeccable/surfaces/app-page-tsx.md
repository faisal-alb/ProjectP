## Direction contract

THESIS: GridFlex is mission control for a grid nobody has ever watched move — an orbital, night-lights view of local flexibility, not a sustainability brochure. Refuses the category default: warm sun/leaf-green "clean energy" marketing, rounded friendly icon cards, stock renewable-energy photography.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black graphite canvas (~#05070d), off-white text, one electric-cyan signal color (~#57D6FF) carried through ambient radial glow washes, the signature power-flow arc, active/hover states, and primary actions — never as flat color blocks. A faint SVG night-grid/line-map texture sits behind key sections. Dark glass panels (translucent graphite, hairline border, soft inner glow) replace white bordered cards. Solana's own purple/green duotone (#9945FF / #14F195) is reserved exclusively for the settlement/on-chain section, visually marking the off-chain (cyan) vs on-chain (Solana colors) boundary the product itself draws. Geist Sans (already installed) for display/body — its geometric, engineered character is deliberately kept as the single family across headings and body, no display-face swap. Geist Mono for every numeric/telemetry readout. Tracked uppercase micro-labels used sparingly for nav and status strips only, never as a kicker sitting above a heading (banned outright). No cards-of-icon+heading+text as page structure: parallel categories (flex-resource types, settlement guarantees) render as single console/instrument panels divided by hairlines, not repeated bordered boxes.

STORY: A utility operator or asset owner lands on a dark orbital view of a city grid at night. A glowing arc sweeps from a battery icon into a pulsing "Downtown — 91% risk" zone and dissolves into calm — they understand in one glance that GridFlex sees exactly where the grid is stressed and moves flexibility there before it breaks, verified and settled transparently on Solana. They scroll through a connected, chase-lit Predict→Procure→Coordinate→Verify→Settle rail (not five identical cards), see flexibility categories as one instrument panel, see the local map as a radar-style zone view with the same glowing arc reused, see settlement rendered in Solana's own colors, then either enroll their assets or launch the live dashboard.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed near-black hero, no eyebrow/kicker above the H1. Thin nav (wordmark left, tracked-caps anchor links, ghost + glowing-pill CTAs right). Large two-line display headline directly under the nav's breathing room, body copy at reduced measure, two CTAs. Behind/beside the copy: a faint night-grid SVG map with 4 status-colored glowing zone points (Downtown/North/West/South, sourced from real zone data) and the signature animated arc tracing from an off-map resource point into the pulsing Downtown point on load. A dark-glass telemetry card (live system-load %, zones table, congestion alert, available flexibility figure) anchors the visual, all real product data, Geist Mono numerals.

FORM: Direction 3 of a 7-candidate ranked list (SpaceX/Starlink literal reading, utility SCADA control room, orbital/satellite night-lights view [assigned], F1 pit-wall telemetry, ATC radar, maritime bridge console, planetarium dome), assigned by dice roll (seed key 418f0751, assigned index 3) against catalog challengers (industrial streetwear, LEGO instructions, passport ephemera, anti-aliased racing league, drum-machine step row, cracktro scroller — all declined on audience/product-clarity grounds). Raises taken from declined challengers: sequential step-illumination chase-light (drum-machine) applied to the Coordinate/dispatch rail; state-color-flood + segmented numeral counters (racing league) applied to telemetry emphasis and active states; depth-layered brightness/drift parallax (cracktro) applied to the background star/grid-dust field. User confirmed this direction over the literal SpaceX-translation alternative, confirmed scope extends to building the /dashboard operate-mode surface in the same world, and confirmed CSS/SVG-authored visuals over sourced photography (no image-generation tool available this session — code-led build, stated once, no comp round).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Secondary surface: /dashboard (Operate mode)

Inherits OWN-WORLD exactly (same tokens, glass panels, Geist faces, cyan/Solana-purple split). Mode is Operate, not Persuade: expression never obscures task, state, or a familiar affordance; brand lives in precise details (the glow, the arc motif reused small-scale, the mono telemetry type) rather than a big expressive hero. Structure: a control-room layout — top bar (wordmark, live global status, zone selector), a main zone-map/radar view (larger GridMapPreview treatment) paired with the Grid Intelligence forecast panel, a live flex market table below, and a row of per-zone status cards. Assembles the existing real-data components (GridIntelligencePanel, GridMapPreview, MarketTable, ZoneStatusCard, CongestionAlert) restyled into the same dark world rather than inventing new data.
